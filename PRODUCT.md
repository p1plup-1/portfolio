# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and design leads at studios, consultancies, and in-house product teams hiring industrial/product design interns. They are screening many student portfolios quickly, usually from a link in an application or a LinkedIn profile, and deciding whether Edward is worth an interview. A second, slower pass happens when a reviewer opens a project to judge process and craft.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Edward Xu, a product and industrial designer studying for an MFA in Product / Industrial Design at Parsons School of Design (class of 2028). It exists to land an industrial/product design internship, though the site itself does not say "looking for an internship". Success means a reviewer quickly understands the kind of designer Edward is, opens a project, sees evidence that they can take a concept from research to a built object, and gets in touch.

## Positioning

Edward builds real things. Their projects go past renders to working physical prototypes, and they pick up new tools and methods quickly. Strong visualization (renders and visual communication) supports the work but doesn't replace it. Other student portfolios can show nice renders. This one should show the whole path from research through concept to a physical build.

## Operating Context

- Reviewers usually arrive from a job application, email, or LinkedIn, on desktop or laptop and sometimes on a phone.
- The Work page is a grid of about eight projects. Each project should lead to its own detail page.
- The About page covers skills (Research & Analysis, Conceptualisation, Development), learning ability, software, and contact.
- Contact goes through email, phone, and LinkedIn. No form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS site: `index.html` (Work), `about.html` (About), `style.css`. No framework or build step.
- Industrial/physical product design only. Digital/UX work is not the focus.
- Software listed: SolidWorks, Fusion 360, KeyShot, Vizcom, Photoshop, Lightroom, InDesign, Premiere Pro.
- Project count: about eight, matching the current grid.
- Open: hosting/deploy target, project titles and order, and the structure of project detail pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Edward Xu. Title line: "Product Design".
- Voice on the About page: plain, first person, short.
- Education: Parsons School of Design, MFA Product / Industrial Design, 2028.

## Evidence on Hand

- Edward has renders (KeyShot/Vizcom), process work (sketches, research, mock-ups, iterations), and photos/video of physical prototypes for their projects. These aren't in the repo yet; all eight project tiles and the portrait are placeholders.
- Contact details are real and live in `about.html`.
- Not on hand: testimonials, awards, press, employer names, or project outcomes and metrics. Don't make any of these up.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the build.** Physical prototypes and making are the main proof. Renders are there to support them.
2. **Process is evidence.** Each project should show research, iteration, and a final object, so a reviewer can see how Edward thinks, not just what they made.
3. **Respect the screener's time.** A reviewer should know what kind of designer this is within seconds and reach a project in one click.
4. **The work leads.** The site stays out of the way of the projects.
5. **Only real claims.** Every outcome, skill, and credential shown must be true and backed by the work.
